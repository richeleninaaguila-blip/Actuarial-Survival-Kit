FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Actuarial Science?

An actuary is a professional who uses mathematics, statistics, and financial theory to analyze and manage risk and uncertainty.

01

Key Responsibilities

Actuaries help organizations answer critical questions such as:

  • How much should insurance cost?
  • What is the financial risk of an event happening?
  • How can companies prepare for future uncertainty?
Professional Bodies: Society of Actuaries (SOA) ↗ Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA) ↗
02

Career Opportunities

What careers can I pursue after graduating?

  • Actuary
  • Actuarial Analyst
  • Risk Analyst
  • Financial Analyst
  • Investment Analyst
  • Insurance Analyst
  • Insurance Underwriter
  • Data Analyst
  • Statistician / Data Scientist
  • Operations Research Analyst
  • Research Scientist
  • System Manager / Auditor
  • Educator
